Styles · 5 of 8

One style per video, two to choose from

hologram

Dark, with a grid and glow. Inter font.

Architecture, code, pipelines and PRs, for a technical audience. It is the default.

sketch

Warm paper and hand-drawn strokes. Caveat font.

Concepts for newcomers, processes and stories, for managers, students or clients.

The same scene works in both. Colors are tokens, but text measures differently: switch the style, rebuild and run check again.

Source: references/styles.md. The stills come from example videos made with the skill (their on-screen text is Spanish).

Quality · 6 of 8

A gate that blocks the export

CheckKindWhat it catches
out-of-boundserrorSomething leaves the 24 px safe margin of the frame
overlaperrorTwo visible elements cover each other
text-overflowerrorText does not fit even at its minimum size
opening-punctuationerrorText that starts with an opening question or exclamation mark
nondeterministicerrorThe same frame, rendered twice, gives different pixels
composition-balancewarningHalf the frame empty, small content, or content sitting too high or low

export refuses to run on errors (--force skips that). A warning is a real finding, not noise. The gate cannot see a wrong arrow or a hidden hero, so you look at the stills.

Source: references/scene-api.md (QA report) and cli/index.ts.

Delivery · 7 of 8

Everything lands in ~/explainers

~/explainers/2026-11-17-<slug>/ ├── scene.js ├── index.html (player with play, scrub and export) ├── <slug>.mp4 ├── stills/ └── explain.json

MP4

To send or upload. 2 Mbps by default, about 15 MB per minute in hologram. --bitrate changes it.

HTML preview

preview opens it. It is self-contained and works offline.

claude.ai Artifact

The skill offers to publish that same HTML as an Artifact, so you can share a link.

The folder is never the current one. EXPLAIN_HOME changes the root.

Source: SKILL.md (intro, Loop steps 7 and 8).

First step · 8 of 8

Start with one command

Ask the agent, for example, "explain how X works in a video", or create it yourself:

First command

bun <skill-dir>/cli/index.ts new my-topic --style hologram
  1. Edit scene.js with the pieces.
  2. Run build and then check until it exits 0.
  3. Look at the stills and export.

Needs Bun and Chrome or Chromium. Nothing to install.

Source: SKILL.md (Setup, Loop) and cli/index.ts --help.
